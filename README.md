FreshMart Supermarket Sales Analysis
Overview
This project analyzes sales transaction data from FreshMart Supermarket, covering January–May 2026 across four branches (Ibadan, Ife, Lagos, Osogbo). The goal was to clean the raw sales data and extract insights on revenue performance, product trends, customer behavior, and payment methods to support business decisions.
Tools Used
• Microsoft Excel (data cleaning, pivot tables, dashboard, formulas)
What I Did:
1. Data Cleaning — Standardized inconsistent text formatting (e.g. capitalization in city and customer-type fields), checked for duplicate order IDs, and verified calculated fields (Sales Amount = Quantity × Unit Price).
2. Pivot Table Analysis — Built pivot tables to summarize revenue by product, category, city, customer type, and month.
3. Dashboard — Created a one-page interactive dashboard with KPIs (total revenue, transactions, quantity sold, average transaction value) and charts for monthly revenue, revenue by category, revenue by city, and revenue by product.
4. Business Insights & Recommendations — Translated the analysis into plain-language insights and actionable recommendations for stock allocation, staffing, and marketing. 

## Key Findings
- Highest-revenue city: Lagos
- Highest-revenue product: Rice 5kg
- Highest-revenue category: Groceries
- Most-used payment method: Cash
- Highest-revenue month: January
- Customer split: Regular customers slightly outspent New customers
